# Scalper V2 additive dashboard

Date: 10 September 2026

## Release scope

- Additive route: `/n50/strategy/trading-analytics?view=scalper_v2`.
- Existing route retained: `/n50/strategy/trading-analytics?view=scalper`.
- No API, database, collector, schedule, signal formula, notification, paper
  order or live-order change.
- UI uses the existing `/v1/trading-analytics/scalper-context` and
  `/v1/trading-analytics/charts` contracts.

## Implemented experience

- Large underlying candlestick chart at left with exact CE and PE stacked at
  right. Every chart owns its price scale and uses green/red candle direction,
  orange EMA9 and an IST time scale.
- Persistent exact-pair rail with 26px CE/PE premiums, selected expiry, current
  OI leaders, compact option chain and At time, Levels, Rules, Measure and
  Health sections.
- CE1/CE2 and PE1/PE2 rank positive current OI by magnitude with deterministic
  strike/contract tie-breaks. Invalid, non-positive and conflicting duplicate
  strikes do not become leaders. The UI labels the retained observation scope;
  it never calls the nearest-pair fallback a full-expiry chain.
- The underlying plot includes in-session rank lines and a proportional
  price-coordinate OI profile. Off-range leaders remain in the rail.
- Four same-page option analytics: OI by strike, signed delta OI by strike,
  observed-scope PCR and combined common-unit max-pain payout distribution.
  Missing baselines stay missing and no provider-native value is called rupee
  exposure.
- Canonical V7 signals are consumed directly. Manual A/B retains exact
  A-open to B-close arithmetic and remains explicitly illustrative/read-only.
- Active 1m/5m/15m/60m data loads first. Once visible, the other exact-pair
  timeframes preload in the background via the existing React Query cache.
  Switching a cached timeframe needs no second cold wait.
- Responsive behavior keeps all three charts readable and stacks the rail and
  analytics at tablet/mobile widths. V2 styling is isolated in its own module.

## Truthful limitations

- The available SmartAPI context can be a retained observed cohort or a nearest
  paired window. The actual scope is disclosed; full-expiry coverage is not
  claimed when absent.
- Delta OI remains unavailable when its canonical baseline is unavailable.
- The max-pain display is a common-provider-unit estimate, not verified rupee
  payout and not a forecast.
- A latest IV observation is not expanded into a synthetic history.
- V2 does not invent an exit. It shows V7 references and manual A/B evidence;
  booked or paper fills remain owned by their existing ledgers.
