# New conclusions you can draw now

## 1. Raw strength vs residual strength
You can now distinguish:
- **stock is strong because the index is strong**
- **stock is strong even after removing index effect**

This is the single most important stock-level upgrade.

## 2. Breakout quality
You can now say whether a breakout is being supported by:
- sustained VWAP control
- persistent residual strength
- normal or above-normal participation through the day
- orderly trend structure rather than a one-bar jump

## 3. Headline spike vs persistent leader
A stock can show a sharp short-term move while still failing the persistence tests.
Now you can separate:
- short-lived spikes
- real leaders that keep holding VWAP and closing well

## 4. Laggards that may catch up
When large-cap breadth is healthy and the stock’s short-horizon residual starts improving, the engine can flag names that are not yet leaders but are improving fast enough to study as catch-up candidates.

## 5. Smoother trend vs noisy trend
Range efficiency and close-location quality let you say:
- smooth trend, likely better continuation quality
- noisy move, more prone to fade / mean reversion

## 6. Better watchlists
You now get dedicated watchlists for:
- residual leaders
- VWAP control breakouts
- headline spikes
- catch-up candidates
- index-beta followers
