# P&L, costs and tax

Actual execution P&L and hypothetical target-track P&L are separate. Charges are effective-dated profiles; no zero-cost fallback exists. Seeded NSE cash and options profiles use the 2026 NSE transaction/STT sources recorded in their rows; the ₹20 brokerage assumption is broker-dependent and must be reviewed when the broker tariff changes. GST, SEBI fees, STT and stamp are itemised.

The default management provision is `max(net realised after trading costs, 0) × 35%`, once at trade-group closure. It is planning evidence, not tax advice. Summaries aggregate ledger entries and never tax again.
