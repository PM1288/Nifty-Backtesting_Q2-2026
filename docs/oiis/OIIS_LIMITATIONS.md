# OIIS limitations and blocked claims

- Current-panel membership causes survivorship bias and prevents rankable
  Rules-of-Engagement status until point-in-time membership is available.
- Phase A is daily cash-equity replay, not OIIS LIVE intraday execution.
- Cash LONG trades are replayed. SHORT scores are retained as signal studies;
  no fictional overnight cash short is executed.
- Outcomes are isolated per symbol. The full scan does not pretend to be a
  ₹16L/max-eight portfolio; capital-constrained cross-symbol replay is a later
  evaluation scenario.
- Options and futures are blocked pending aligned point-in-time chain, Greeks,
  margins, lots, costs and owner-approved risk rules.
- Public volume and delivery are participation proxies, not proof of any named
  institution's activity.
- No calibrated probability or profitability claim is permitted.
- Event history is not sufficiently qualified to award a positive catalyst
  score across the complete window.
- Broker order placement is absent. Historical, read-only, paper and shadow
  authority do not imply live-order authority.
