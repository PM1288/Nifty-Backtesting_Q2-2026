# Paper Trading Evidence Workbench V2 operator guide

1. Start in **Overview**. Read each lane badge before comparing numbers. Booked, open, observed, hypothetical and simulated lanes have different accounting meaning.
2. Use **Analysis Context** to set period, entry strategy, status, direction, horizon, accounting class, capital basis and gross/net basis. The URL updates so the investigation can be copied or restored.
3. Open **Trade Evidence** for the authoritative ledger. Choose a column preset for speed or **All fields** for complete evidence. Comfortable, Dense and Audit modes use the same canonical rows.
4. Select any table row, chart point, attention item or timeline allocation to open the same trade inspector.
5. In the inspector, use **Economics** to compare accounting lanes and **Calculation Trace** to inspect formulas, source fields, time basis, capital basis and policy ownership.
6. Use **Path Through Time** for date/event inspection, **Reward & Pain** for MFE/MAE and target conversion, and **Factor Analysis** for point-in-time entry factors versus later outcomes.
7. **Capital Recycling** and **Scenario Analysis** are simulations. They do not overwrite booked paper execution.
8. **Methodology & Audit** explains data sources, missing-data treatment, immutable events, limitations and formula versions.
9. **Export view** produces raw CSV values plus filters, as-of time, environment, policy version and the accounting warning.
10. Admin-only comments and quality reviews remain server-authorised and audited.
